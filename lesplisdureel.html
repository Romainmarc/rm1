Les plis
du réel

Book design 134 pages
Hard cover
100g/m2 uncoated paper
14,8x21cm

MF Fondation and MF Corinthe designed by @milletypefoundry

Les plis du réel, depicting a short journey, a road trip from France to Austria by three characters looking for adventure, exploring through a shared passion. More important than the end goal or destination is the journey itself, which is presented in this book aiming to put lights on what graffiti often fails to highlight. Through a logbook and photographs, the characters present an intimate view of this adventure for the purpose of personal documentation.
