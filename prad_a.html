Prad'a

Graphic Design Design: Tabaramounien
Scenography: MJ Studio

The PRAd'A (Palmarès Rédional d'architecture en Nouvelle-Aquitaine) is a prize list aimed at rewarding architectural creations in the nouvelle-aquitaine region. The identity system inspired by the classification into different categories: S, M, L, XL. Based on adaptability, games of scale and construction revolving around a variable typography offering a playground.
